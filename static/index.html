Larger models

The full ASR family supports richer transcripts with speaker labels and timestamps. The streaming variant adds continuous transcription as speech arrives.

Could become: searchable interviews, meeting archives, or live captions.

The two full transcription models were about 17 GB each when checked. They aren’t downloaded or running in this project.

From transcripts to meeting notes

Product idea

A transcript is the foundation. Useful summaries, decisions and action items need another reasoning step, plus a way to check every claim against the source.

Could become: a meeting companion with context you can trust.

Automatic summaries, meeting search and calendar integrations are outside this prototype. The notebook interface explores the product’s feel.

Long-form, multi-speaker audio

Research history

The original VibeVoice-TTS research explored long conversations with multiple voices. Microsoft subsequently removed that TTS code from the repository.

It is part of the project’s research story, not an available feature in this app. The smaller Realtime model above is the speech runtime tested here.
